### `visualize_filter.py`

Visualize effect of filter on a Dirac-delta peak, i. e.  a single filled 
element.

### `visualize_lengthscales.py`

visualize length scale violation, i. e. places in the design where details are
smaller than a sphere of a given radius which resembles the desired lengthscale.

### `volume_conservation.py`

Short demonstration to check volume conservation after filtering.


### `morphology_filters.py`

Apply the morphology based filters by Sigmund (2007) (dilate, erode, open, 
close, open-close, close-open) to a black and white test design with features 
larger and smaller than the filter radius and compare to the density filter.

### `morphology_beta.py`

Show how the dilate/erode filters converge to the exact maximum/minimum 
operators with increasing beta along a line profile through a bar.
